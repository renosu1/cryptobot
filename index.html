      Keep running in the background

      Normally the bot runs while the Trend Rider window in Terminal is open. Background mode keeps it running with no window and starts it again when you log in. Your Mac still pauses it while asleep; the bot catches up when it wakes.

      Stop Trend Rider

      Stops the bot and the dashboard until you open Trend Rider again. Nothing is sold.

      While stopped, the bot makes no decisions. Your paper account is saved. Stop now?

      Live trading with Kraken Beta

      Real money can be lost. In testing on 2023–2026 prices, this bot made less than just holding (+195% vs +383%), while losing less in crashes. No bot can promise a profit. Run paper trading for at least a month first, and only use money you can afford to lose. You must be 18 or older to use an exchange.

      Real money

      The stocks account is paper-only for now. In testing on 24 stock histories, trend rules cut the worst crashes but made less money than simply holding in every one, so there is no rush to trade it for real. A broker with a trading API (such as Alpaca) could be connected later.
